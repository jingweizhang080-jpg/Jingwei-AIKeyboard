JingweiAIKeyboard V0.2.1 local pinyin lexicon

The bundled app/src/main/assets/pinyin_lexicon.tsv is generated from:
1. Rime luna_pinyin dictionary:
   https://github.com/rime/rime-luna-pinyin
2. Rime simplified essay vocabulary:
   https://github.com/rime/rime-essay-simp

Both upstream repositories provide their license terms in their LICENSE files.
Copies of the relevant license text are included in this directory.

This V0.2.1 uses the Rime lexicon data only; it does not yet embed the native
librime engine. Pinyin access is wrapped behind PinyinEngine.java so native
librime can replace the local lookup backend in a later version.
